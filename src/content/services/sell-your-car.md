Whether your car is old, damaged, unregistered or simply unwanted, we can give you a quote and arrange pick-up. Tell us about your vehicle — make, model, year and condition — and we'll get back to you.

## What We Buy

- Old or high-mileage vehicles
- Damaged or accident-affected vehicles
- Unregistered vehicles
- Vehicles that are no longer running

## What to Tell Us

- Make, model, year and whether it's currently registered
- Whether it's drivable, and the general condition
- A few photos, inside and out, if you can manage it

## Payment & Pick-up

Payment is made by bank transfer or EFT, in line with current Australian regulations on vehicle purchases. Once you accept a quote, we'll arrange a pick-up time that works for you.
