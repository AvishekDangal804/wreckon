We can arrange removal of your vehicle as part of a sale or wrecking job. Let us know your location and vehicle details and we'll confirm the details with you.

## When Removal Is Included

Removal is arranged alongside a [vehicle sale](/services/sell-your-car) or [wrecking job](/services/wrecking) — it isn't offered as a separate standalone service.

## What to Tell Us

- Your vehicle's location (suburb or address)
- Whether the vehicle is drivable
- Make, model and year

## What Happens Next

Once your sale or wrecking job is confirmed, we'll arrange a pick-up time directly with you.
